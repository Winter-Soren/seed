# Current state

Updated 21 November 2026. Follow PLAN.md and AGENTS.md.

## What we know

All three original A genomes improved their food choices within a stationary lifetime. Among creatures reaching both meals, first-to-twentieth meal accuracy rose by 27, 21 and 25 percentage points, with standard errors near two points. They struggled after a food-rule change. A fixed-weight recurrent baseline won the original average score in all three seeds.

E116 found smaller evolutionary steps beat larger ones, but neither justified replacing the original genomes. E117 tested a different kind of inheritance. Newborns given donors' acquired connection changes reached shelter 38.4, 44.6 and 37.3 turns sooner than fresh-start siblings, with paired standard errors of 5.3, 5.4 and 5.4 turns. Survival increased in all three lineages. Most arrivals happened before cold, leaving the specific content of the archive open.

E118 let all three original genomes mutate for ten generations with the persistence trait active. Their descendants saved −6.6, −8.5 and +5.0 turns versus their parents, with paired standard errors of 6.0, 6.4 and 6.9 turns. All met the survival safeguard and none met the shelter improvement threshold. All ten updates per lineage and all held-out results passed independent reconstruction.

E119 continued the three saved E118 genomes from generation ten through generation twenty. Every generation received real acquired weight changes from its parent cohort. Against its immediate parent, the final descendant saved −2.1 ± 3.1, −40.8 ± 6.3 and +6.3 ± 6.0 turns. No lineage cleared the improvement threshold. All three descendants reached shelter more slowly with their accumulated archive than when the same genome started fresh. Absolute survival was low.

E120 gave newborns a movie of a practiced forager before they acted alone. Teachers chose food on about 81–83% of late encounters. Newborns chose food first in 67, 66 and 64 of 128 solo lives. Watching beat every control in zero of three lineages. The independent reconstruction confirmed the decision.

## What is running

E121 is registered. It asks whether a fixed reader can tell which color is food from the teacher's public movements in new movies. Its decision concerns the movies. Newborn behavior was measured separately in E120. Publication remains on hold at the owner's request.

## What is next

Run the E121 benchmark and all three held-out panels on the laptop. If the movies reliably reveal food color, train observers to use them. If the reader fails, test a controlled demonstration whose visible choices can be verified. Register each comparison before opening its worlds.
