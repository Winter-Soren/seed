# Animal learning tasks for Seed

The next target is **reversing an acquired preference and using the new preference in a fresh place**. Seed’s stable-world food choices improved during life. An engineered memory also let a lesson survive three social handoffs, but retaining that memory hurt all three lineages when the food rule changed. The comparative question is which behaviors Seed can reproduce under controlled conditions. Its current experiments do not assign it the intelligence of an animal species.

| Animal experiment | Finding | Decision for Seed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medicinal leech | While feeding, leeches responded less to the same moderate touch. The study traced this change to reduced transmission from sensory neurons. [Gaudry and Kristan (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19801989/) | Hold the test display and body state fixed while changing past experience. Otherwise hunger or the current scene can mimic a change in preference. |
| Fruit fly larva | After the relationship between odor and outcome changed, larval choices reflected the second association and, in some conditions, retained an influence of the first. [Mancini et al. (2019)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31615854/) | Establish the old preference first. Then measure how far the same creature switches, against a matched creature whose rule stayed fixed. |
| Adult fruit fly | During reversal, omission of an expected punishment engaged a reward pathway. This differs from receiving an explicit positive reward. [McCurdy et al. (2021)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33602917/) | Seed receives actual food or poison in E127. An agent that passes that test would still need an omission-only test before we could claim sensitivity to missing outcomes. |
| Honeybee | Bees trained to choose a matching or nonmatching stimulus transferred the relation to unfamiliar stimuli. [Giurfa et al. (2001)](https://doi.org/10.1038/35073582) | Changing the grid tests transfer across layouts. A later relational task would need unfamiliar stimuli and an unrewarded first choice, with position and identity counterbalanced. |
| Bumblebee | Five of fifteen observers acquired a two-step box-opening sequence after exposure to trained bees. The first action carried no direct reward for the observers. [Bridges et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07126-4) | A later social target is a pupil completing a novel two-action sequence alone. A demonstration, an exposure-matched shuffled sequence and a no-demonstration control would distinguish the order of actions from simple attraction to a location. |

## Architecture decision before looking at E127

Two independent reviews considered changing the controller and changing the behavioral test. Keep the existing controllers in E127. Both architectures receive the same contradictory experiences, both initial rule assignments and a common fresh world. The local choice probe measures all five actions, so approaching neither item stays visible. A comparison with normal lifetime updates and with Hebbian changes disabled tests what those updates contribute. [Registered design](E127_PROTOCOL.md)

A computational mushroom-body study offers a more complex alternative. Jiang and Litwin-Kumar trained recurrent output circuits with compartment-specific teaching signals. Different units carried different signals while the population expressed reward prediction error. This shows that diverse teaching signals can support the modeled tasks. Seed already has a global modulator, and its single-coefficient forgetting hypothesis makes a cheaper, distinguishable prediction. A positive result for either design in another model supplies no performance estimate for Seed. [Computational study and model](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009205)

- If at least two plastic lineages establish the old preference and **still prefer the old food** after sixteen reversed contacts, the leading mechanism to test separately is a single feedback-gated decay coefficient. A harmful contact would pull recently active learned connections toward their birth values. Compare it with ordinary learning, matched constant decay and the same controller with the added decay switched off. Require preserved stationary learning as well as better reversal. This is a software hypothesis. A fly’s omission circuit is not evidence that this particular equation works in Seed.
- If a lineage switches locally but fails in the fresh grid, the next experiment should vary search distance with memory and cues held fixed. Search, elapsed time, and the change of scene can each account for the loss. Adding a forgetting rule would leave those explanations unresolved.
- If the old preference never forms in the chamber, first test whether guided versus self-chosen actions can establish the association. Faster forgetting cannot demonstrate reversal of a preference the assay never established.
- If the same lineages pass both tests, the next harder measurement is retention after a neutral delay and transfer to novel cue arrangements. A two-color preference on new layouts falls short of a honeybee-style matching rule or the bumblebee’s two-step social task.

E126’s six generation-50 checkpoints stay sealed under its original cost decision. E127 uses different controllers and fresh diagnostic worlds. Any follow-up needs its own preregistration, three independent lineages, resource forecast and unchanged controls.
