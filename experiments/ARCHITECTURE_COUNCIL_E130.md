# Architecture roundtable · 22 November 2026

**Decision before E130's final worlds.** Keep the registered founder comparison intact. Use its failure modes to choose a competing controller. The two research chats exchanged proposals in two rounds. The implementation reviewer checked what signals Seed already exposes. The proposals below are candidates, with no performance claims.

| Candidate | What it would change | Strongest objection and decisive control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploration after a prolonged absence of food | A bounded counter per animal would temporarily increase exploratory moves after a run of unrewarding actions. The baseline and challenger receive the same local view and realized energy changes. | Extra random moves alone could help. Compare with equal-budget exploration at outcome-independent times as well as the unchanged controller. Report actual overrides per live turn, deaths before feedback and stationary performance. Replay identical choices and outcomes before testing preference with exploration disabled to distinguish improved sampling from improved learning. |
| Plasticity gated by locally detected contact | Permit the existing Hebbian update after the creature's own action produces evidence of contact. Prediction continues. The previous local view and executed movement can indicate an encounter. | Seed already gates learning using energy change and prediction error. Test whether the new timing adds anything at matched update frequency. Use matched births, fixed histories and fresh free-choice probes. More plasticity or fewer damaging updates in general could otherwise explain a gain. |
| Compact context memory | Maintain a small acquired state about the current contingency. Test the first choice of an alternative whose outcome the creature has yet to observe after the switch. | A handcrafted one-bit solution can exploit perfect complementarity in a two-color world. A fitted recurrent model adds training cost. The existing social reader already holds a scalar belief. First compare with a chosen-option-only update and a memoryless controller under the same observations and training allowance. |

The first chat favored exploration if agents go long enough without informative encounters. The second favored presenting voluntary, balanced choices as a diagnostic before changing the controller. Both agreed to distinguish the opportunity offered from the item actually sampled. They also agreed that a larger recurrent model has weak justification on the present two-color task. The balanced-offer diagnostic must include sham placements on the same schedule and a solo probe, so changing the location of cues cannot by itself win the test. Mice in Parker and colleagues' experiment chose between two available alternatives. Seed's ordinary grid adds a search problem. [Mouse comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4882228/) · [State inference and the untried alternative](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16899731/)

The implementation review found that ordinary metabolism changes energy on empty turns and that energy is clipped at the cap. A nonzero energy change cannot serve as a reliable contact detector. The pending neural update uses the previous sensory activity and actual executed move. A contact gate can therefore use information already in the controller before the old activity is overwritten. Any proposed exploration override must feed the move actually taken back to the controller. Both changes are smaller than adding another recurrent layer. Benchmark wall time and verify the disabled path against the original trajectories before drawing a cost or performance conclusion. [Existing neural update](../seed/seed.py#L147)

### How the council will use E130

The following measurements select a design for a separate experiment. E130's primary scientific decision stays as preregistered. Use only the independently audited archives. For generation-200 creatures in the reversal condition, report the number alive at turn 150 and these quantities over turns 150–199. Keep the four originally assigned animals and both initial rules within the independent family for uncertainty estimates.

- Count a 25-turn gap only when the animal is alive both before and after every turn, has no item contact, and the entire gap fits inside the 50-turn window. Check the first 25 turns and the 25 turns immediately after the first poison. Count an animal at most once.
- Count creatures that encounter both a food and a poison in the window. These are simulator outcome labels. The agent only receives its ordinary observations, and energy clipping can make the observed change ambiguous.
- Report death before any post-switch contact, death before the switch, and the original-assignment and switch-alive denominators. These traces do not record whether an uneaten item was visible.

If at least two lineages establish the old preference, miss the complete recovery gate and at least 20% of switch-alive creatures have the defined gap, prioritize the bounded exploration controller. If that condition is absent and at least two such lineages encounter both outcomes in at least half their switch-alive creatures, prioritize a locally evidence-driven learning update. If neither pattern predominates, use controlled access to distinguish missing feedback from ineffective updating before assigning a cause. A positive E130 joint result would move the target to successive reversals. These thresholds guide allocation of research time. No candidate earns adoption until it beats a matched baseline on a separately registered, fresh three-lineage comparison with survival safeguards and measured runtime.

Use `python experiments/analysis/e130_architecture_feedback.py --self-check` for the hand-counted fixtures. After the final audit, run `python experiments/analysis/e130_architecture_feedback.py --audit results/diagnostics/E130-independent-audit.json --out results/diagnostics/E130-architecture-feedback.json`. This reads the audited event files and uses families for standard errors, including for ratios conditional on being alive at the switch.

### Independent benchmark and eventual release

A useful external memory test is MiniGrid Memory. The agent sees an object, travels down a corridor and must choose the matching object at a junction. A fair Seed entry needs an explicit adapter between the public observations/actions and its sensor/motor interface. Give every controller the same information and training allowance. Prohibit access to the hidden target, future layout or private environment state. Publish exact environment versions, report transfer without retraining separately from retrained performance, and count timeouts. Compare against a memoryless policy and a recurrent baseline on held-out layouts. Seed has not been scored on this task. [Official MiniGrid specification](https://minigrid.farama.org/environments/minigrid/MemoryEnv/)

A future Hugging Face release can package the inherited genomes, compatible development and simulation code, evaluation commands, license and model card. Include the observed failure cases and uncertainty, plus a reproduction from a clean checkout. Public distribution is a separate owner decision.
