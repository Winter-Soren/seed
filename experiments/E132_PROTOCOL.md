# E132 · Can voluntary access to both alternatives help a creature relearn?

E131's independent audit found that the registered exploration controller failed its behavioral gate in all three E103 lineages. We retire that setting. Time to first food combines searching and choosing. E127 established that matched, guided encounters can reverse preference. Here we change the distance to a pair of alternatives while the original controller chooses every action. This is a one-creature diagnostic on the path between the guided assay and the four-creature world. No new controller is fitted.

## Assignment and intervention

Use the original E103 A0, A1 and A2 generation-200 genomes. A family shares birth, per-block placement randomness, ordinary action keys and final probe worlds across conditions. Each family receives **both initial rules**, with equal weight. Use four development families with seed 2026121100 and 64 independent final families per lineage with seeds 2026121101, 2026121102 and 2026121103. Derive disjoint family streams using tag 0xE132 and independent roles for development of the brain, geometry, exposure actions and the solo/readout apparatus. Training generations are zero.

Use the ordinary E127 one-creature brain, body and sensors in the 16-by-16 world. Each block lasts eight voluntary ticks. At every block boundary replace the sparse presentation grid with one A and one B. Consumed items stay absent until the next block. No regrowth during exposure, forced motion, quota, demonstrations or reinforced resets. The pair remains fixed within its block. Keep the body, energy, actual-action feedback, prediction and Hebbian updates across blocks. Use the same random action stream in paired copies. Divergent actions are expected.

All copies first experience eight near blocks under their initial rule. Put one A and one B at distinct uniformly chosen cardinal neighbors relative to the live creature at the start of the block. Clone after these 64 ticks. From the common state run four continuations of eight blocks each. Cross **near vs sham** with **reversed vs stationary** rule. The comparison starts before action and consumption on tick 64. In the near arm use the same near presentation rule. In the sham arm sample two distinct offsets uniformly without replacement among the 255 noncentral cells of the torus, allowing chance adjacency. Assignment of A and B to offsets is random and independent of the rule and reward in both arms. Changing the supply of reachable items can change survival, experience and energy. Record actual intake for both colors. The manipulation is access to a pair in a sparse apparatus.

To measure old-rule preference immediately before the split, process the final acquisition feedback on a **discarded copy** with one voluntary empty tick, then measure local choice. Preserve the actual pre-split state for the four continuations. After the final block, give each living continuation one voluntary tick on an empty grid to process the preceding outcome. In a discarded copy, use the established 12 ordered adjacent placements to measure the probability of choosing the currently safe color minus the other color, with all five actions available and learning off during this readout. Give a separate copy the standardized living solo body and an ordinary fresh full-density grid for a 50-turn first-item probe, ordinary learning on and no offers. The two readouts start with identical W, activity, predictor and topology. Freeze dead exposure states, carry dead assignments at zero on all subsequent scores and never revive an animal to score its choices. All comparisons include every assigned birth.

## Frozen decision

For each initial rule and family, score first food in the 50-tick solo probe as +1, first poison as −1 and death before contact or no contact as zero. Average across a family's two rules, form within-family differences, then report the mean and sample standard error over independently assigned families within each lineage. Use direct contrasts with a paired standard error. Report separate food, poison, no-contact, exposure survival, solo survival and local preference, including by initial rule. Record both item identities actually contacted after the split and signed realized energy feedback, including clipped gains. The primary exposure measure is the fraction of all assigned lives making at least one contact with each color during the 64 continuation ticks. Contacts leading to death count.

A lineage passes only when all these conditions hold.

1. Common pre-split preference for the old food and late near-reversal preference for the new food each exceed two standard errors above zero. The local measure includes a zero for every dead animal.
2. Reversal near minus sham increases the fraction sampling both colors by at least 0.10 and more than two paired standard errors.
3. On fresh solo reversal worlds, the near arm's signed first-item score exceeds two standard errors above zero. Near minus sham is at least 0.05 and exceeds two paired standard errors. The difference between that gain and the corresponding gain on stationary worlds exceeds two paired standard errors. Each solo grid uses the last rule of its exposure phase.
4. The paired lower bound (mean minus two standard errors) for near minus sham is greater than −0.05 for stationary signed solo performance, and for end-of-exposure survival in each schedule. End of exposure is immediately after the last of the 128 presentation ticks. Report survival after the following empty feedback tick separately. A death on that tick scores zero in both subsequent readouts.

The access diagnosis requires all four conditions in all three lineages. Report each failed component. A full pass would support a contribution from easier access to the alternatives in this apparatus and motivate an autonomous search design. It would leave the individual contributions of calorie intake, visual stimulation and feedback unresolved. If both colors are sampled more but the behavioral gate fails, investigate how feedback is used before adding search capacity. If the exposure manipulation itself fails, close this assay without inferring a memory defect. No adjusting distances, timings, number of blocks, seeds or thresholds on these worlds.
