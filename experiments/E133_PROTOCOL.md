# E133 · Can a contact gate protect relearning?

E127 established reversed choice under sixteen matched encounters. E128–129 found that subsequent empty-world updating could weaken an acquired preference and that an imposed pause partly rescued transfer. E130–131 found no replicated autonomous recovery, including with outcome-timed exploration. E132 increased exposure to both colors by 68–73 points but did not establish reversal-specific recovery, and exposure survival declined. A weak endpoint cannot identify forgetting without a preceding acquired preference. The council chose one local gate on the current Hebbian rule. Further search changes would change evidence again. A fitted context state has weak justification here because one deterministic outcome can reveal the two-color rule by design.

## Decision

First average both initial rules in each independent family. Compute sample SE over families, using paired differences for comparisons. Each lineage must pass every criterion. Positive means strictly above two SE. A gain must meet its listed minimum and strictly exceed two paired SE. A safeguard requires the lower two-paired-SE bound to exceed −0.05.

1. Common old local S is positive. Immediate reversal S in both native and contact and delayed reversal S in contact are at least 0.10 and positive. Immediate contact-minus-native reversal S meets the safeguard.
2. Contact delayed-minus-immediate reversal S meets the safeguard. The contact-minus-native difference in that change is at least 0.025. Delayed contact-minus-reopened reversal S is at least 0.025. Delayed contact-minus-equally-sparse reversal S is at least 0.05. Apply the paired gain rule to all three.
3. Delayed contact reversal Y is positive and improves by at least 0.05 over each of native and equally sparse. Report stationary-adjusted differences descriptively. Delayed stationary S and Y meet the safeguard against both controls. Delayed solo end survival meets it in each history against both controls.

Credit retention only if both immediate acquisition checks and the delayed comparison pass. Replicate in all three lineages for an architecture advance. If immediate preference is absent, prioritize use of signed feedback. If retention improves but transfer misses the gate, report the local effect and leave this design out of evolutionary training. If equal sparsity or reopening fails to distinguish the mechanism, retire this test setting without tuning on these assignments. A full pass would justify a separately registered autonomous deployment check, then bounded equal-budget selection of a single heritable between-contact learning gain versus the native controller. Compare each descendant to its parent and matched training control on new ordinary worlds where it must find its own feedback. Preserve the existing formal reversal and evolution gates.
