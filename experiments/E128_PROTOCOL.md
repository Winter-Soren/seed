# E128 · Does the revised food preference last through an empty interval?

E127 showed that all three original plastic lineages could reverse a food preference after guided encounters and use it immediately in a fresh grid. E128 tests the duration of that learning on new worlds. The only new experimental factor is the number of active turns in an empty world after acquisition. Use the same three saved E103 plastic controllers and matched E113 recurrent controllers. There is no evolution, new brain mechanism or observer training in this test.

## Experience and comparisons

Use the original [E127 acquisition procedure](E127_PROTOCOL.md). Both starting food rules are tested in every family. Balance the old rule's eight or sixteen pre-boundary encounters, then give sixteen more encounters with the reversed or continuing rule. Preserve both histories. Keep the pre-boundary old preference as a descriptive check.

After acquisition, keep the whole living creature, including its position, energy, last energy change, time, neural activity, weights, predictor and mask. Replace only the grid with an empty torus and the random stream with the registered retention-action stream shared across histories. Observe the state immediately, after one turn and after 33 turns of this same trajectory. The creature chooses its own actions. Ordinary energy use, motor feedback, recurrence and lifetime updates continue. No items appear. The final interval includes changes of scene, energy and activity. A decline would establish a cost of that experience; its cause would require a separate comparison.

At each time, probe discarded clones with E127's twelve adjacent cue arrangements and its five-action readout with both weight updates frozen. Separately put the unprobed neural state in a standard fresh body and an ordinary grid using the reversed food rule. Use the same target layout, initial position, readout and action randomness across times and histories. Score the first contact within fifty turns for every assigned life. Report food, poison, no contact and survival. Independent uncertainty comes from families. Average placements and the two initial food rules within each family before any standard error or paired contrast.

## Decisions fixed before measurement

Let S be the probability of moving toward the currently safe item minus the probability of moving toward the other item. Let C be S after reversed feedback minus S after stationary feedback, both scored against the same current food. In the grid, G is the probability of first finding food within fifty turns. Y is +1 for first food, −1 for first poison and zero for no contact or death before contact. Report mean and one standard error for all three times. The one-turn snapshot is descriptive.

For **local retention** in one lineage, the reversal-history S and the paired history effect C must each be at least 0.10 and greater than two standard errors at both zero and 33 turns. Also compute the family-paired difference in reversal-history S at 33 minus zero. Its mean minus two paired standard errors must be strictly greater than −0.05. A wide interval crossing the allowed loss leaves retention unestablished.

For **retained grid performance**, reversal-history Y must exceed two standard errors at zero and 33 turns. At 33 turns, reversed minus stationary G must be at least 0.10 and greater than two paired standard errors. Reversal-history G at 33 minus zero, averaged within family, must have a mean minus two paired standard errors strictly above −0.05. Report absolute success in addition to the history contrast.

The local milestone requires **all three** plastic lineages to meet the local criterion. The combined milestone requires those same three to meet both the local and grid criteria. Report each lineage and any partial success. Apply the same calculations to the fixed-weight controls. A controller without an immediate preference has no demonstrated preference whose persistence this assay can measure. The 0.05 margin is a prospective tolerance in probability units. Its choice and the three-of-three rule are deliberately more demanding than E127's immediate two-of-three gate.
