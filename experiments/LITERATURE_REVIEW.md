# Literature review for Seed's next decisions

Research review finalized for the E113 council decision, 19 November 2026. Literature and critical discussion only; not a preregistration or authorization to run. Main chat owns gates, implementation, compute and publication of this review. This review changes no experiment order.

**Recommendation:** complete Phase 2's fair nonplastic recurrent baseline first. Retain only two conditional mechanism candidates: evolvable lifetime weight decay for failed reversal training, and a genome-controlled eligibility trace for failed delayed-reward training. Neither is approved to implement now. Differentiable plasticity, consolidation and new growth/pruning rules remain deferred.

## Evidence that needs explaining

E112 source is `c224f91e20d9ea12a09613d70320cfe17abea8cb`; its eight panels were published at `594acfe1ff75ced3bbc26a7a91645ff64bdcd203`, tagged `v0.2-evalv3`. The A results below come from saved-genome `summary.json` files on the same 256 held-out world families. Each ± is one world-cluster standard error for a fixed genome, not uncertainty across evolution seeds.

The local rule-knowing greedy reference scores 82.278 ± 1.364 stationary and 69.024 ± 0.700 with reversal. Its privileged rule information and different action choices prevent attributing that entire gap to learning speed or navigation alone. Matched-meal improvements remain conditional on reaching meal 20. A single switch does not measure retention of earlier, still-useful knowledge, and slow reversal is not itself evidence of catastrophic forgetting or exhausted learning capacity.

Sources: [A0 summary](../runs/20261119-030209_E112-v3-A0/summary.json), [A1 summary](../runs/20261119-030336_E112-v3-A1/summary.json), [A2 summary](../runs/20261119-030424_E112-v3-A2/summary.json); broader reporting in [EVAL v3](../results/EVAL_V3.md). No evaluation worlds were rerun for this review.

Current [brain_step](../seed/seed.py) computes `m = tanh(10*a*denergy + 10*b*surprise + c)`, with surprise a squared observation-prediction error. It applies a masked four-term local activity rule to the previous activity, then clips weights at ±3. There is no longer eligibility trace or lifetime weight decay. Squared sensory surprise is unsigned; it is not a reward-prediction error. However, the evolved coefficient and total modulator can have either sign, so the equation alone does not show incorrect punishment handling. ES's outer optimization regularization is a different operation from within-life weight decay.

E112 has structural plasticity **off**. E107's specific activity-growth/pruning intervention changed fixed-A fitness by −2.591796875, −1.423828125 and −0.5078125, respectively, without reported SEs. These are adverse point estimates, not a statistical rejection of every structural-learning method. E109 stopped at 62/200 with no final evaluation. See the preserved E107/E109 entries in [LOG](LOG.md).

## Focused paper evidence

Costs below are implementation-level estimates for Seed, not measured runtimes. Every proposed test is deferred to its authorized stage. A paper's success on its own tasks does not establish benefit here.

## Two conditional candidates

**1. Evolvable within-life weight decay, only after failed rung-1 training.** Hypothesis: persistent old preferences impede recovery, and modest decay permits faster replacement. A minimal conceptual intervention is `W_next = clip((1-d)*W + current_local_update)` on existing legal connections, with one genome-controlled coefficient and exact legacy behavior when disabled. This is a proposed engineering test, not a conclusion from the papers or E112.

The main chat should preregister the range, timing and inactive/dead/frozen behavior before development, keep modulator sign handling unchanged, and preserve old genome/protocol compatibility. Compare unchanged-architecture training to decay-enabled training at matched budgets, then lesion decay in each learned controller on matched fresh worlds. Retain stationary performance, encounters, survival and all-agent post-switch accuracy alongside fitness. Total-weight decay also weakens birth weights and changes recurrent dynamics; any gain could arise from less saturated action selection or more exploration, rather than removal of a stored rule. Weight-bound occupancy and update directions can help distinguish these hypotheses if registered as diagnostics. No privileged flip signal or reset is allowed. Do not claim retention without the later retention task.

Reject the hypothesis if the declared rung gate or necessary lesion fails. A weak result is not permission to combine decay with a sign change. Incremental arithmetic is O(N²) per tick with no necessary new N² state; training still costs a three-seed comparison. Numerical times remain unknown until benchmarked.

**2. A genome-controlled eligibility trace, only after failed rung-2 training.** Hypothesis: delayed energy feedback is credited to intervening actions because the current update remembers only the immediately preceding activity. A minimal candidate averages the existing masked local update signal into `e_t = lambda*e_(t-1) + (1-lambda)*u_t`, then gates it with the correctly aligned feedback. `lambda=0` should reproduce current credit timing exactly when the feature is enabled without memory; the fully disabled branch must preserve legacy behavior. This normalized choice is a proposal to preregister, not a fitted recommendation.

Use hand-calculated development sequences to establish which action a delayed reward actually credits, including distractor actions and positive/negative feedback. Define item-outcome delivery and subsequent brain-consumption steps precisely; delaying only the modulator while energy observations reveal the outcome immediately would leak feedback. Tracing the entire four-term proposal includes its constant term, unlike tracing coactivity alone; choose explicitly before implementation. Compare trace vs zero-memory lesion at fixed k=5, and include the immediate-reward control; evolve each required arm from blank under the same budget. Fix update order and scaling first: an unnormalized accumulating trace changes effective step size as well as memory, while normalization attenuates individual events. Register a gain-matched instantaneous development control and record update norms/clipping before attributing improvements specifically to temporal credit. A long trace may blend conflicting actions or carry pre-reversal credit forward. Reject if the rung gate or trace lesion fails; do not lengthen it based on held-out results.

With N=157, one float32 N×N trace is 98,596 bytes per agent; four agents × eight worlds × 128 candidates would be about 385 MiB of trace state if all are materialized concurrently. This is arithmetic, not a measured JAX peak. Temporaries, compilation and any autodiff tape cost extra. ES requires no lifetime gradient tape for this candidate. Benchmark with the existing guards before full training.

## Council review: Phase 2 comparator, 19 November 2026

**Conclusion adopted in E113: direct encoding fits Phase 2 with recurrent state and feedback retained. Call it a fixed-weight recurrent baseline, not a non-learning agent.** E113's preregistration and subsequent council-resolution entry in [LOG](LOG.md) now specify encoding, raw fixed weights and the stop rule. Implementation and delegated development verification are underway; this review does not certify their outcome or report a training result. The same LOG records Phase 1's hosted CI passing 91 tests in 260.769 seconds at publication commit `594acfe1ff75ced3bbc26a7a91645ff64bdcd203` ([CI run](https://github.com/Winter-Soren/seed/actions/runs/44404157089)); that is Phase 1 verification, not E113 validation.

[RL²](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.02779), §2, explicitly distinguishes slowly trained weights from a fast learning procedure represented in recurrent activations. [Wang et al., Learning to reinforcement learn](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.05763), §§2.2–2.3, independently demonstrates reward-driven adaptation with frozen weights. The latter uses recurrent dynamics to implement a learned update procedure exploiting the task distribution; it resets state between new tasks, not between every decision. Both are counterexamples to the premise that unchanged weights imply unchanged behavior or an inability to learn. Their gated recurrent architectures and gradient-based outer training do not establish that Seed's tanh recurrence and ES will discover such a procedure.

### Registered design and remaining interpretation limits

| Issue | Minimum specification and interpretive limit |
| --- | --- |
| Encoding | E113 specifies `54*(157-1)=8424` legal weights: every nonsensor target receives all sources except itself. Normal initialization has **standard deviation** `1/sqrt(156)` (variance `1/156`). It retains the 103 clamped observations, 48 hidden and 6 motor units, two microticks, action-logit scale, sampled-action efference copy and communication output. |
| State and feedback | Initialize recurrent state once per life and retain it across decisions. Reset between independent worlds/lives; never reset at an undisclosed rule flip. Keep energy and energy-change observations with exactly the same clipping and timing as A. These are the available feedback, not an added privileged food label or ideal reward channel. No online predictor, weight or structural update may remain. |
| Development | Direct encoding implies family members share the same weights; A's shared genome produces different sampled wiring. Record this difference instead of adding unapproved development noise. Four relatives sharing a world remain a single statistical cluster even with identical weights. |
| Bounds | E113 explicitly uses finite, raw, unbounded direct weights and a separate tick path bypassing all weight/predictor updates and clipping. Values above 3 must remain bit-identical throughout life; there is no birth projection. Record initial/final maximum absolute weight and fraction above 3. This resolves the unintended first-tick clipping concern while retaining an explicit asymmetry: plastic A weights are clipped to ±3. The fixed-weight invariant remains a verification requirement. |
| Search budget | Keep the proposed ES settings, population 128, eight worlds, 200 generations and three seeds as PLAN requires. This is 54.3 times as many genome coordinates as A, with dense connectivity rather than A's sampled sparse wiring. Equal sigma, learning rate and per-coordinate L2 do not equalize functional perturbations, representation or optimization difficulty. At initialization sigma 0.1 is about 1.25 times the direct weight SD. Record these facts; do not tune the baseline after seeing held-out results. |
| Compute | Match environment-interaction/generation budget and report actual time and peak memory separately. Larger optimizer state and a different evaluator require a fresh operational benchmark; matched generations are not a promise of equal wall time. Compute worker alone owns released jobs. |

### Smallest useful measurement set

1. **Primary outcome:** stationary food-minus-poison fitness per initial agent, each seed separately, with world-family SE. E113 fixes pairs A0/direct0, A1/direct1 and A2/direct2, using E112's immutable A event arrays and the same EVAL v3 worlds/random streams. Final evaluation follows generation 200; there is no held-out tuning or favorable seed selection. Matching training seed numbers alone does not establish correlated training outcomes, even though the world-key schedule is matched.
2. **Comparison uncertainty:** retain per-world fitness differences for each registered direct/A pair and report their mean and paired SE, `SD(d_world)/sqrt(n_worlds)`. Report all three evolution outcomes; 256 world families for one saved genome do not estimate uncertainty over evolution. Three seeds provide limited evidence about optimizer reliability. Do not infer a difference from overlapping/nonoverlapping separate error bars alone.
3. **Learning diagnostics:** reuse EVAL v3's ordinal-meal accuracy, matched meal-1/20 change, encounters and survival. Its existing reversal panel can be reported descriptively with post-switch bins, without training on reversals or promoting to Phase 3 early. Keep all-agent and final-survivor quantities distinct. These measures compare behavior; they do not by themselves isolate recurrent memory as its cause. Early/late improvement can reflect changing exposures and survival, even with a matched cohort.
4. **Validity checks:** identical observation/action/environment semantics and no weight/predictor updates; independent life initialization; complete denominators and input/source provenance. Do not add a hidden-state-reset lesion to the core comparison merely to call it nonplastic. If a later claim specifically attributes behavior to recurrent memory, preregister that diagnostic separately; resetting only hidden units also leaves action feedback and environmental memory, so it is not a fully memoryless control.

**Council decision now preregistered in E113:** for each seed pair, compute per-world `A_i - direct_i`, its mean `delta_i`, and paired `SE_i = SD(d_world)/sqrt(256)`. Continue beyond Phase 2 only if **all three** completed, validity-clean comparisons satisfy `delta_i > 2*SE_i` after the fixed budget. Otherwise stop for owner input. A direct advantage beyond two paired SE favors the baseline; insufficient separation is **unresolved**, never proven equivalence. Report point-estimate baseline wins and uncertainty plainly. No practical-equivalence margin, majority exception or post-result hyperparameter repair is claimed. This is E113's explicitly registered decision rule; Phase 3 retains its own separate gates.

If the continuation criterion fails, headline the baseline advantage or unresolved comparison as appropriate. If it passes, the claim is an advantage over this particular direct recurrent controller under matched ES settings—not proof that fixed-weight networks cannot learn, or that lifetime weight changes alone caused the advantage. No result here quantifies progress toward an undefined general-intelligence percentage.

**Strongest remaining counterargument:** E113 compares complete designs, not an isolated plasticity intervention. The direct controller changes search dimensionality, density, developmental variation and amplitude constraints together with lifetime weight updates. In particular, failure of ES to find a good 8,424-weight recurrent policy within A's 155-parameter search budget would not show that such a policy is unavailable. The all-three gate controls the declared continuation decision but cannot remove this causal limitation. Conversely, a direct win could reflect freer connectivity or weight amplitudes rather than a universal advantage of fixed weights. These are predeclared limits on interpretation, not reasons to alter the registered experiment now.

This addendum adds one primary work (Wang et al.) to the initial nine-paper set. It changes only this review; no implementation, test, training or publication action was performed.
