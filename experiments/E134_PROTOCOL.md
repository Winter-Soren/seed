## Held-out assignments and decisions

Use 128 fresh independent families per lineage, thirty-two in each held-out `(old contacts, gap)` cell. Balance sham mapping sixteen/sixteen within each cell. Average the two initial mappings and the twelve local placements within a family, then construct each full contrast within that family. The SE is its sample SD divided by sqrt(128). Related creatures, histories, clones and placements are repeated observations within one independent family. Report every lineage separately.

Positive means strictly greater than two SE. A gain must also reach its numerical floor. A safeguard requires the paired mean minus two SE to exceed −0.05. Let D denote descendant, P parent, R reversal, T stationary, I informative and U sham. The local history effect H compares the reversed and stationary states while scoring both toward the new food.

1. Descendant pre-boundary old-food S is positive. Its informative final reversal S and H each reach 0.10 and are positive. Its informative reversal S exceeds its parent's by at least 0.05 and more than two paired SE.
2. `[(D.S_R − P.S_R)_I − (D.S_R − P.S_R)_U]` reaches 0.025 and exceeds two paired SE. Require the exact sham history identity as an integrity check. This accompanies the direct and absolute criteria.
3. Descendant informative solo reversal Y is positive. Define `g_R = D.Y_R − P.Y_R` and `g_T = D.Y_T − P.Y_T`. Both `g_R` and `g_R − g_T` reach 0.05 and exceed their two paired SE.
4. Apply the safeguard to descendant-minus-parent pre-boundary old-food S, informative stationary final S and Y, and final solo survival in each informative history.

The advancement claim requires all components in all three independently trained lineages. Report each failed or inconclusive component without adjusting seeds, attempts, checkpoints, distances, gaps or thresholds. A local-only gain has its own limited interpretation. An incomplete or invalid run leaves the scientific decision open.

At assumed paired SD 0.50, 64 families give SE 0.0625 and a two-SE hurdle 0.125. The fixed 128 give SE 0.0442 and hurdle 0.0884. Under a normal approximation a true 0.05 gain clears that hurdle about 19% of the time in one lineage, while a 0.125 gain clears it about 80%. The joint gate and adjusted contrast can be harder. At SD 0.25 the hurdle is about 0.044. These prospective assumptions illustrate limited sensitivity to small gains. No held-out outcome chose the fixed count.

A one-bit record of the latest informative color/outcome could pass in this deterministic complementary world. A Hebbian-specific attribution requires a faithful lesion. An attribution to the reversal objective requires equal-budget stationary-objective selection. A causal feedback attribution for the solo gain requires solo sham clones as an additional control. E134's claim is inherited improvement in feedback-dependent local choice and improved free solo performance after matched histories.
